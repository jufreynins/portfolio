Web Systems

Systems Designed Around Real Workflows

A useful web system should help people manage records, complete tasks, monitor activity, and make decisions more efficiently.

Example Concepts

The systems below are concept designs that show the kind of internal tools I can build — not existing client or production systems. Each is clearly labeled with its current status.

Business Productivity System

Task Management System

Concept

A system for assigning, tracking, and completing team tasks without relying on scattered spreadsheets or chat threads.

Main Users
Small business teams and project coordinators
Business Problem
Tasks get lost across email, chat, and spreadsheets, making it hard to see what is actually in progress.
Key Features
  • Task assignment & due dates
  • Status tracking (To Do / In Progress / Done)
  • Team member views
  • Activity history
  • Priority tagging

Technology direction: TypeScript, structured data model, role-based views

Operations & Inventory System

Retail & Inventory Management System

Concept

A system for tracking stock levels, managing product data, and keeping inventory counts accurate across sales channels.

Main Users
Small retail businesses and shop owners
Business Problem
Manually tracking inventory in spreadsheets leads to overselling, stockouts, and inaccurate product counts.
Key Features
  • Stock level tracking
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Product catalog management
  • Order history
  • Basic reporting

Technology direction: TypeScript, structured data model, dashboard UI

Healthcare Practice System

Dental Clinic Management System

Concept

A system for organizing patient appointments, treatment records, and daily clinic schedules in one place.

Main Users
Dental clinic staff and administrators
Business Problem
Paper-based or disconnected scheduling makes it hard to track appointments, patient history, and daily clinic capacity.
Key Features
  • Appointment scheduling
  • Patient records
  • Treatment history
  • Staff calendar view
  • Automated reminders

Technology direction: TypeScript, calendar/scheduling UI, structured records

Internal Admin System

Business Admin Dashboard

Concept

A centralized dashboard for viewing business data, managing content, and monitoring activity without digging through separate tools.

Main Users
Business owners and internal teams
Business Problem
Important business information is scattered across separate tools, making it hard to get a clear, current picture.
Key Features
  • Data overview widgets
  • User and role management
  • Activity logs
  • Searchable records
  • Reporting views

Technology direction: TypeScript, dashboard UI, role-based access
