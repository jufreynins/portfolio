    Good to know

    About these formats

    A quick primer on JPG, PNG, and WebP, and how this tool works.

    Convert either direction

    Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP files and convert to any of the three — including WebP back to JPG or PNG when you need broader compatibility or an editable format.

    Why WebP usually wins

    At equivalent visual quality, WebP typically produces smaller files than JPG or PNG, especially for photos and complex graphics — which means faster page loads and better Core Web Vitals.

    Everything happens locally

    This tool decodes and re-encodes your images entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your files are never uploaded, so they never leave your device.

    When to pick JPG or PNG instead

    Use JPG for maximum compatibility with older software, or PNG when you need lossless quality or transparency support. PNG output ignores the quality slider since it never loses data.
