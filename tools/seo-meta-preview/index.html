Good to know

Getting the most out of your snippet

A quick primer on search snippet length and how this tool works.

Why length matters

Search engines truncate titles and descriptions that run too long, cutting them off with an ellipsis. Keeping both within a safe range helps your full message reach searchers.

Desktop vs. mobile

Mobile search results generally show less text than desktop before truncating. Check both views here so your snippet still makes sense either way.

These are guidelines, not guarantees

Google sometimes rewrites titles and descriptions automatically, and the real cutoff is based on pixel width, not a fixed character count. Treat this preview as a helpful approximation.

Everything happens locally

Your title, description, and URL are only used to render the preview in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server or stored anywhere.
